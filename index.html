DotLauncher privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

DotLauncher is a home screen app for Android. This policy explains what it reads on your phone and what happens to it.

The short version

DotLauncher has no internet access. It does not ask Android for the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Nothing it reads leaves your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What DotLauncher reads, and why

Your installed apps

Why: To show and open them, group them into circles and sort them by letter.

Where it stays: On your phone, in memory.

Which apps you open from DotLauncher, and when

Why: To show your most used apps first, and the right ones for the time of day.

Where it stays: On your phone, in DotLauncher's private storage.

Your home screen layout and settings

Why: To remember how you set things up.

Where it stays: On your phone, in DotLauncher's private storage, and in your phone's own Android backup if you have backup turned on.

Notifications (only if you allow notification access)

Why: To show badges on apps, the sender and first line of your newest messages at the top of the home screen, and to clear a notification when you swipe it away there. It also sees which media is playing, for the skip-track gesture.

Where it stays: In memory only, while the notification exists. Never saved.

Your calendar (only if you allow it)

Why: To show the time and title of your next event under the clock on the home screen and on the screensaver.

Where it stays: In memory only. DotLauncher never changes your calendar.

Your contacts (only if you allow it)

Why: To find people when you search, so you can call or message them.

Where it stays: In memory only, while you search.

A photo you choose (only if you pick one)

Why: To set it as your wallpaper.

Where it stays: Android keeps it as the wallpaper. DotLauncher doesn't keep a copy.

DotLauncher can also open Android's own uninstall confirmation, settings pages and other apps when you ask it to.

Sharing

DotLauncher shares nothing with anyone, because it cannot connect to the internet.

Backups and export

If Android backup is turned on, your layout and settings are included in your phone's backup, which Android stores under your Google account. You can also export your layout to a file you choose. That file only contains your layout and settings, never notifications, calendar events or contacts.

Children

DotLauncher is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

Contact
